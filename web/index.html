Receiver

Bind puts the receiver into bind mode. Then choose Bind in the radio's ExpressLRS Lua script: the receiver takes on the radio's bind phrase and keeps it. There is no way to type a bind phrase into the receiver over this link; to set one without the radio, reflash the receiver from the Receiver firmware tab.
Use the radio model's Receiver number when Model Match is on in the ExpressLRS Lua script, or Off when it is not. The receiver stores the ID; load the receiver settings to see what it currently holds. A mismatch looks like a receiver that sends nothing: radio on, no link, LED showing the WiFi pattern.
To update the receiver's firmware or change its bind phrase, see the Receiver firmware tab.

Receiver settings

The settings the ExpressLRS Lua script shows for the receiver, read over the serial link. Needs ExpressLRS 4.0 or newer on the receiver. Changes are stored by the receiver straight away.

Update the receiver's ExpressLRS firmware

The dongle passes the ExpressLRS flasher straight through to the receiver, the way a flight controller does. Use this to update the receiver, change its bind phrase or change its options.

  1. Switch your radio off. With it on, stick data gets mixed into what the flasher reads, and the flash fails or sits on "Connecting".
  2. Check the receiver has power. On the button version the ring should be lit or blinking, not dark. On the lite version it is on whenever the dongle is plugged in.
  3. Free the serial port. Only one page can use it at a time.
  4. Open the flasher: the ExpressLRS Web Flasher in this browser, or the ExpressLRS Configurator app.
  5. Choose what to flash. Pick the ExpressLRS version that matches your transmitter module (the major version must be the same), then your receiver, for example "HappyModel EP1/EP2 2.4GHz RX" or "RadioMaster RP3 2.4GHz RX". Enter your bind phrase.
  6. Choose the Betaflight passthrough method and start the flash. When asked for a serial port, pick ELRS Sim RX.
  7. Wait for it to finish. The status light shows a blip and two flickers while the dongle is passing data through. About 10 seconds after the flasher lets go of the port, the dongle returns to normal.
  8. Come back here and Connect. Switch the radio on and the link should come up. If you use Model Match, set it on the Controller tab.

If it does not work

What you seeWhat to do
"Wrong target selected" or "Target mismatch, flashing cancelled" The receiver reported a different firmware name from the one being flashed. This is expected when it still has older firmware. Check you picked the right receiver, then flash again and choose to flash anyway.
"Cannot detect RX target", then "Connecting..." without end The flasher is not reaching the receiver. Check the radio is off and the receiver has power, then try again. If it still fails, update the dongle's own firmware on the Dongle firmware tab.
"Serial Receiver UART is inverted" The dongle's firmware is too old. Update it on the Dongle firmware tab.
A warning about the crystal frequency Harmless. The flasher estimates the receiver's clock from serial timing, which is slightly off through the dongle.
The flash stopped partway The receiver's bootloader is still intact. Switch the receiver off and on (or replug the dongle) and flash again.

Without the dongle: a receiver left powered for about a minute with no radio starts its own WiFi hotspot, named "ExpressLRS RX" with password expresslrs, and can be updated from the page at 10.0.0.1.

Flash log
Manual method, no WebUSB needed
  1. Download the UF2 above.
  2. Put the board in update mode: or hold BOOT on the board while plugging it in. A drive named RPI-RP2 appears.
  3. Copy the UF2 onto that drive. The board reboots into the new firmware.

Windows: updating from this page needs the WinUSB driver bound to "RP2 Boot (Interface 1)" with Zadig, once. See docs/WINDOWS.md. The manual method needs no driver.

"Do not show the RPI-RP2 drive" starts the bootloader without its drive, so no Explorer window opens. The manual method cannot be used in that mode, and on Windows the bootloader may need the WinUSB driver installed again for it. If the dongle is not found, replug it and it starts normally.

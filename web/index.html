Default: channels 5 to 16 each give three buttons (low, mid, high). In ELRS Hybrid or Wide switch mode, channels 15 and 16 carry LQ and RSSI; set buttons 30 to 35 to "none" or use Full Res 16ch.

Firmware

Pick a firmware, then step 1 puts a connected device into update mode. Step 2 opens the browser's USB picker: choose "RP2 Boot".
Manual method, no WebUSB needed
  1. Download the UF2 above.
  2. Hold BOOT on the board while plugging it in (or use step 1 above). A drive named RPI-RP2 appears.
  3. Copy the UF2 onto that drive. The board reboots into the new firmware.

Windows: the WebUSB route needs the WinUSB driver bound to "RP2 Boot (Interface 1)" with Zadig, once. See docs/WINDOWS.md. The manual method needs no driver.

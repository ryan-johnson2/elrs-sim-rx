Four wiring stages, each tested before the next goes on, so a fault can only be in what you just added. Hole numbers refer to the map; ticks are kept in this browser.

Unplug USB before moving any wire. The RP2040's pins are 3.3 V only: nothing from a + rail may ever land in rows 1 to 9. The only + rail connections are the ones listed here.

Board map

5 V switched 5 V ground (black wire) GP0 to receiver receiver to GP1 control

Dashed lines are flying leads to the receiver and the button. Go by the red and blue lines printed on your board's rails, not by which column they are in. Everything sits in rows 1 to 27, clear of the mid-board rail break some 830-point boards have.

BENCHBefore the breadboard

Three parts need a soldering iron before they can touch a breadboard, and the receiver should be proven on its own first. You need a multimeter with a continuity beeper for the whole build.

REFERENCEWhen something is wrong

SymptomLook at
No USB device at allCharge-only cable. Then hold BOOT while plugging in: if RPI-RP2 appears the board is fine and needs flashing.
Web page cannot see the portChrome or Edge only, on the HTTPS address. Close anything else holding the COM port, such as PuTTY or a second tab.
USB picker is empty when flashing from the pageWinUSB is not bound yet: Device Manager shows RP2 Boot with a warning icon. Do the Zadig step, or copy the UF2 to the drive by hand.
Ring never lightsLED leads reversed. Q3 the wrong way round. A PN2222 from the kit in place of a 2N7000. A 2N7000 that is weak at 3.3 V gate drive: swap in another from the pack.
Ring always onQ3 source and drain swapped; its body diode then conducts regardless of GP4.
frames_ok stays 0, frames_crc_err or uart_overruns climbSwap the green and blue leads (h1 and h2).
No frames at all, ring on fast flickerReceiver unpowered, or in WiFi mode: it goes there by itself after about 60 s without a radio link. rxpower off then rxpower on.
Stage 4: receiver never powersMeasure Q1 gate (row 15) to ground: about 5 V when off, near 0 V when on. Stuck at 5 V means Q2 or its wiring. Correct gate but no output means Q1's pins are not where you think; trace the adapter again.
Stage 4: receiver stays on after rxpower offQ1 source and drain swapped (its body diode feeds the receiver), or R1 missing.
USB drops when the receiver switches onInrush into C1. This is one of the open questions in docs/BRINGUP.md; note what you see.
Channels 15 and 16 wanderHybrid or Wide switch mode puts LQ and RSSI there. Use Full Res 16ch or set buttons 30 to 35 to none.

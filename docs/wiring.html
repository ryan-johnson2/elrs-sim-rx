ELRS Sim RX wiring guide

Phase 1 breadboard / perfboard build
Matches docs/HBOM.md and firmware/src/board.h as of 2026-11-09
Crossed serial. The receiver's TX pad goes to GP1 and its RX pad goes to GP0. If no frames arrive, these two are the first thing to swap.
Receiver is off by default. R1 holds Q1 off until the firmware drives GP2 high, so the EP2 only powers up when asked.
Every ground symbol is one net. On a breadboard, run U1 GND to the − rail and U1 5V to the + rail, then land each ground and 5 V connection there.

U1 · RP2040-Zero, top view, USB-C up

All seven connections are the top two pins on the left and the top five on the right. Pin names are printed on the underside of the board; confirm against them. GP16 drives the onboard RGB LED and needs no wiring.

Q2, Q3 · 2N7000

Flat printed face toward you, legs down: source, gate, drain. A BS170 looks identical but is the other way round.

Q1 · AO3401A (SOT-23)

Top view, marking readable. Two legs on one side are gate and source; the single leg opposite is drain.

Check on the real parts before wiring

  • EP2 pad order varies by batch. Go by the labels printed next to the pads, not by position.
  • FILN lead colours are not documented here. Find the switch pair with a continuity test (beeps only while pressed); the other pair is the LED, and it only lights one way round.
  • SOT-23 adapter numbering differs between adapter boards. Follow the copper from each MOSFET leg to its header pin.

Build it in stages

The table is ordered to match docs/BRINGUP.md. Test after each stage so a fault can only be in what you just added.

  1. RP2040-Zero alone (1, 2). Flash the firmware; check it appears as a gamepad and a serial port.
  2. Button and LED ring (3 to 9). The ring should follow the led field in status; a short press toggles receiver power.
  3. Receiver, powered directly (10 to 14). For this stage only, run the receiver's 5V pad straight to the 5 V rail. Confirm frames arrive and sticks move.
  4. Power switch (15 to 21). Move the receiver's 5V pad from the rail to Q1 drain. rxpower off should drop the pad to about 0 V.

Before plugging in USB

  • Multimeter on continuity: 5 V rail to ground rail must not beep.
  • C1 stripe (−) is on the ground side.
  • Q2 and Q3 face the way the pinout on page 2 shows.
  • The receiver is not resting on bare metal; wrap it in heat shrink or tape.

Do first, off the board

Update the EP2 over its WiFi hotspot and bind it to your radio from a plain 5 V supply, so you know the receiver works before the dongle is involved.

If the LED ring is dim or dead

A 2N7000 only gets 3.3 V on its gate here, which is near the limit for some parts. Swap in another one from the pack before suspecting the wiring.
